About MarunnUndo

Empowering Kerala with accessible healthcare information.

MarunnUndo (Malayalam for "Do you have the medicine?") was born out of a simple need: to make finding local pharmacies effortless. During emergencies or late-night medicine runs, locating an open pharmacy with stock can be stressful.

We built this platform using open mapping solutions to guarantee your privacy while giving you real-time proximity-based results. Contact pharmacies directly via WhatsApp, get precise driving directions, and save crucial time when it matters the most.
